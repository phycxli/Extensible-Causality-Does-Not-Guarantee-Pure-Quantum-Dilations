For the coefficient table, use the short letters
\begin{equation*}
 (\ell_0,\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3)
 =(L_{+,0},L_{+,1},L_{-,0},L_{-,1}),
 \qquad p_{j,rc}=(P_j)_{rc},
\end{equation*}
where $j\in\{+,-\}$ and $r,c\in\{0,1\}$ label the projector and its qubit block.
Adjacent letters are multiplied in the written order. Alice letters are moved
before Bob letters using only interparty commutation; multiplication order
within each party is retained. No projector relation is reduced before the
coefficient comparison.

The following finite word sets fix a complete basis for this expansion:
\begin{align*}
 \mathcal A&=\{1\}\cup
 \{\ell_{2f+o}^{\dagger}\ell_{2f+o'}:
           f,o,o'\in\{0,1\}\},\\
 \mathcal P&=\{p_{j,rc}:j\in\{+,-\},\ r,c\in\{0,1\}\},\\
 \mathcal B&=\{1\}\cup\mathcal P
                    \cup\{bb':b,b'\in\mathcal P\},\\
 \mathcal W&=\{ab:a\in\mathcal A,\ b\in\mathcal B\}.
\end{align*}
The numerical flag $f=0,1$ denotes $+,-$, respectively; $o,o'$ are the two
Alice output indices. The symbols $a,b,b'$ in the set definitions stand for
words, and $1$ is the empty word. Thus $\mathcal A$, $\mathcal B$, and
$\mathcal W$ contain $9$, $73$, and $657$ distinct formal words.

The nonzero scalar contractions generate $448$ distinct words before their
coefficients are combined. Of these, $244$ cancel within $\mathcal Q$ and
have zero coefficients in both $\mathcal H$ and $\mathcal I$. The other
$204$ words are all listed in Table~\ref{tab:hall-sos-coefficients}: $48$
belong to the support of $\mathcal H$, and $156$ have zero target coefficient
and opposite $\mathcal Q$ and $\mathcal I$ coefficients. The remaining $209$
words in $\mathcal W$ are not generated. Equivalently, every one of the
$453$ words in $\mathcal W$ omitted from the table has Target, Q, Ideal,
and Residual all equal to zero. This specifies the coefficients on the
entire basis, including all words outside the target support.

For each word $w$, the Target, Q, and Ideal columns give its coefficients
in $-\kappa\mathcal H$, $\mathcal Q$, and $\mathcal I$, respectively, and
Residual is Q plus Ideal minus Target. All entries are unscaled integers:
the common factor $1/\kappa$ belongs to the SOS weights, not to this table.
The notation $[X]_w$ denotes the coefficient of the word $w$ in a
polynomial $X$.
